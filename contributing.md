# Contributing - Open Arsenal Sound Specifications

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Open Arsenal Sound Specifications. Contributions from innovators, researchers, engineers, audio professionals, developers, and the open-source community are welcome.

## Contribution Guidelines

Contributions should support the development, improvement, and expansion of open-source sound and audio technologies. All submissions should align with the project's goals of modularity, interoperability, accessibility, privacy, security, and human control.

### Areas of Contribution

- **Specification Development:** Propose new specifications or improve existing specifications covering speech recognition, speech synthesis, audio processing, voice intelligence, music generation, acoustic engineering, spatial audio, and related disciplines.
- **Research and Innovation:** Contribute research, methodologies, technical concepts, and documented findings that advance sound and audio technologies.
- **Modular Architecture:** Recommend improvements that strengthen modularity, extensibility, interoperability, and compatibility across different systems and hardware.
- **Audio Processing:** Develop concepts for sound enhancement, noise reduction, distortion correction, audio analysis, and signal processing.
- **Speech and Voice Technologies:** Contribute specifications for speech recognition, speech synthesis, transcription, translation, voice identity, and conversational audio.
- **Music and Creative Audio:** Propose improvements for music generation, sound design, adaptive audio, audio synchronization, and creative applications.
- **Acoustic Engineering:** Contribute research and specifications involving sound propagation, acoustic environments, spatial audio, resonance, vibration, and sound measurement.
- **Privacy and Security:** Improve requirements for audio data protection, encryption, consent, biometric privacy, secure processing, and user-controlled data management.
- **Accessibility:** Help develop inclusive audio technologies supporting multilingual communication, hearing accessibility, speech assistance, and adaptable user experiences.
- **Documentation:** Improve explanations, terminology, examples, use cases, and specification clarity.

## Submission Standards

All contributions should:

- Clearly explain the proposed change and its intended purpose.
- Follow the project's modular specification approach.
- Use consistent terminology and clearly defined requirements.
- Avoid unnecessary duplication of existing specifications.
- Identify dependencies and interoperability considerations where applicable.
- Address privacy, security, accessibility, and user consent where relevant.
- Distinguish established capabilities from proposed or experimental concepts.
- Include supporting research or references when making technical claims.
- Preserve compatibility with the project's open-source licensing requirements.

## Specification Development Principles

Contributions should encourage reusable, adaptable, and interoperable technologies rather than isolated proprietary implementations.

Core functionality should remain within the appropriate core modules. Optional capabilities, integrations, and specialized functionality should be organized as optional plugin modules.

Specifications should support different hardware configurations, software environments, processing architectures, and AI systems wherever practical.

Audio collection, recording, analysis, processing, storage, and transmission should incorporate appropriate privacy protections, security safeguards, consent mechanisms, and human oversight.

## Community Participation

Contributors are encouraged to participate in discussions, propose improvements, identify gaps, report inconsistencies, and collaborate on new specifications.

Constructive feedback and alternative approaches are welcome. Contributions should remain focused on improving the quality, usefulness, accuracy, and accessibility of the specifications.
